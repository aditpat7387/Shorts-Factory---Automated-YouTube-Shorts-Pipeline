
The great Youtuber Plan

1. The Anti-Demonetization Rule (Crucial First Step)

YouTube strictly enforces its Reused and Inauthentic Content policies. To get approved for monetization, human reviewers must see clear original value:
• Add your voice or face: Record a quick 3-second intro hook, narrate the context of the play, or include a small picture-in-picture reaction/breakdown.
• Create narrative edits: Combine 2–3 clips into a "Top Plays" or "How This Streamer Outsmarted Everyone" micro-story rather than uploading single raw stream moments.
• Heavy visual transformation: Use zoom-ins, sound effects, on-screen tactical breakdowns (arrows/circles), and custom captions so the video looks distinctly different from the original stream.

2. Dual-Audience Content Plan (India + US)

Targeting both regions requires balancing language, game selection, and upload timing so the algorithm pushes your Shorts to both audiences.

Game & Creator Selection

• Cross-border PC/PS titles: Focus on games huge in both regions—GTA V (RP & online stunts), Valorant, Fortnite, Call of Duty: Warzone, Minecraft, and major PlayStation exclusives (like God of War or Spider-Man boss fights/easter eggs).
• Creator mix: Feature globally recognizable streamers (like Kai Cenat, IShowSpeed, Shroud, TenZ) alongside crossover Indian PC/PS creators (like Techno Gamerz, Scout, Mortal, or BeastBoyShub).
• Language strategy: Use simple, energetic English for voiceovers and bold on-screen captions. English is understood by the core PC/PS gaming demographic in India while remaining native to US viewers.

Weekly Posting & Timing Schedule

• Volume: Post 1 to 2 Shorts daily (consistency builds algorithmic trust faster than batch-uploading).
• Best Upload Window (IST): Upload between 7:30 PM and 9:30 PM IST. This catches Indian viewers during peak evening scrolling and hits US East Coast audiences around 9:00 AM–11:30 AM EST as they start their day.

High-Performing Short Formats

1. "What Went Wrong / IQ Breakdown" (30–45 sec): Show a clutch play or fail, pause right before the climax, explain the mechanic or mistake in 5 seconds, then play the result.
2. "PC vs. PS5" or "Pro vs. Noob" Comparisons (20–30 sec): Split-screen two streamers reacting to the same game moment or mission.
3. Trending Gaming News & Leaks (15–25 sec): Overlay gameplay clips while summarizing major GTA 6 updates, PlayStation showcase announcements, or patch nerfs.

3. Step-by-Step Monetization Strategy

Because US Shorts RPMs are significantly higher than Indian RPMs, your goal is to use high-volume views from India to hit eligibility fast, then maximize revenue through diversified streams.
• Phase 1: Hit the 500-Subscriber Tier
	• Reach 500 subscribers + 3 million Shorts views in 90 days.
	• Unlock Super Thanks and Channel Memberships. Encourage viewers to leave Super Thanks to request specific streamers or games for future breakdowns.
• Phase 2: Unlock Full Ad Revenue (1,000 Subscribers)
	• Hit 1,000 subscribers + 10 million Shorts views in 90 days. (Note: YouTube has announced this threshold will increase to 20 million Shorts views starting February 1, 2027, so aim to qualify before then).
	• Avoid copyrighted music: Never use trending commercial songs from the Shorts audio library if you want maximum ad payouts—music licensing cuts heavily into your 45% revenue share. Stick to game audio and royalty-free background beats.
• Phase 3: High-Margin Off-Platform & Affiliate Income
	• Affiliate links (Pinned comments / Channel bio): Set up both Amazon Associates India and Amazon US (OneLink). Link the controllers, gaming mice, headsets, and keyboards used by the streamers featured in your clips.
	• Long-form compilation funnel: Once a week, stitch your best 15–20 Shorts into an 8-to-10-minute "Best PC & PS Moments of the Week" video with full commentary. Link this long-form video using YouTube's "Related Video" feature inside your Shorts—long-form videos earn much higher ad rates per view, especially from US viewers.
